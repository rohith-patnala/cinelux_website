Case 01 / Living lounge

Performance without visual clutter.

The brief is to keep the room welcoming for daily use while improving the experience for films, sport and music.

  • Display size selected around seating distance
  • Speaker positions aligned with room layout
  • Equipment and cable routes planned discreetly

Outcome target: a flexible room that feels like a living space first and an entertainment space whenever needed.

Case 02 / Compact cinema

Make every seat count.

A smaller room benefits from careful geometry: sightlines, screen scale, speaker angles and bass management must be considered together.

Instead of treating the room as a scaled-down commercial cinema, we shape the setup around the available depth, the primary seats and the desired level of immersion.
