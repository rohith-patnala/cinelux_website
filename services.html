Service questions

Know what to expect.

Every home is different, so the scope is confirmed after we understand your space.

Can you install a surround system in an existing room?

Yes. We review the room and available cable routes, then propose the least disruptive practical layout.

Do you supply equipment as well as install it?

We can specify a system and discuss equipment supply as part of the project scope.

Can you upgrade just the audio?

Absolutely. We can focus on sound quality while keeping compatible parts of your current setup.
